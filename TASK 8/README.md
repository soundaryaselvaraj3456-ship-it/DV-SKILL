# * Student Performance Analysis

# * Project Overview

This project analyzes student performance data to understand how factors such as parental education, lunch type, and test preparation affect students' academic scores. The analysis uses Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to perform data analysis and visualization.

# * Dataset

* Dataset: Student Performance Dataset
* Number of students: 1000
* Main subjects:

  * Math
  * Reading
  * Writing
* Categorical factors:

  * Gender
  * Race/Ethnicity
  * Parental Level of Education
  * Lunch Type
  * Test Preparation Course

#  Objectives

 Compare average student scores across different parental education levels and lunch types.

 Analyze score variation between students who completed test preparation and those who did not.

 Identify relationships between mathematics, reading, and writing scores.

 Calculate correlations between different subject scores.

 Identify educational inequalities and performance gaps.

 Provide data-driven educational equity and policy recommendations.

 Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

#  Data Analysis

**Grouped Bar Chart**

A grouped bar chart was created to compare average Math, Reading, and Writing scores across different parental education levels and lunch types.

### Key Outcome

Students with different parental education backgrounds show differences in average academic performance.

Students receiving standard lunch generally have higher average scores than students receiving free/reduced lunch.

The visualization helps identify potential socioeconomic performance gaps.

Test Preparation Analysis

Box plots were created to compare score distributions between students who completed the test preparation course and students who did not.

### Key Outcome

Students who completed the test preparation course generally achieved higher scores.

Test preparation is associated with improved academic performance.

The box plots also show the spread and variation of scores within each group.

#  Scatter Plot Analysis

Scatter plots were created for the following subject combinations:

- Math vs Reading
- Math vs Writing
- Reading vs Writing

### Key Outcome

 Math and Reading scores show a strong positive relationship.

Math and Writing scores also show a strong positive relationship.

Reading and Writing scores show the strongest positive relationship.

Students who perform well in one subject generally tend to perform well in the other subjects.

Correlation Heatmap

A correlation heatmap was created to measure the relationship between Math, Reading, and Writing scores.

### Key Findings

Math and Reading have a strong positive correlation.

Math and Writing have a strong positive correlation.

Reading and Writing have the strongest correlation among the three subjects.

The heatmap provides an easy visual representation of the strength of relationships between subjects.

# * Educational Equity Analysis

The analysis examines performance differences based on socioeconomic and educational factors.

### Key Findings

# * Lunch type is associated with differences in student performance.

# * Parental education level shows differences in average student scores.

# * Students who completed test preparation generally perform better than students who did not.

# * These patterns indicate that access to educational resources and support may influence academic outcomes.

# * Policy Recommendations

# * Provide free or low-cost test preparation programs for students.

# * Provide additional tutoring and academic support for students with lower performance.

# * Improve access to learning materials and educational resources.

# * Support students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

# * Monitor achievement gaps based on socioeconomic factors.

# * Introduce reading and writing support programs because reading and writing performance are strongly related.

# * Conduct regular student performance assessments to identify students who need additional support.

# * Expected Outcomes

# * Improved academic performance among students.

# * Reduced achievement gaps between socioeconomic groups.

# * Better access to educational resources.

# * Increased participation in test preparation programs.

# * More data-driven educational decision-making.

# * Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that student performance is influenced by several educational and socioeconomic factors. Test preparation is associated with better scores, while lunch type and parental education levels reveal potential educational inequalities. The strong relationships between Math, Reading, and Writing scores also show that improvement in one academic area may be accompanied by improvement in others.

The findings can help schools and policymakers design targeted academic support programs and promote greater educational equity.
